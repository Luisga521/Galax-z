ON THOSE PAGES

STOP
empties the page · stopped already: all of them
REC
on and playing: records its notes and dial turns
REC+−+DIAL
that dial's recording goes
KEYagain
lets a kept note go (EUCLID, GROOVE)
Recorded dials last until power-off. GROOVE was red in v11–v12.

SHIFT→PROJECT

SWITCHING ON

SCREEN
update mode: install Galax-z OS, or go back to TE's
TRACK
content mode: the disk, to put a backup back
SCREEN+STOP
factory reset: erases your own content
A boot animation plays on the keys after the start v2; two Quindar tones sound v11.

WITH THE GALAX-Z APP

TEMPO+SCREEN
the OP-Z's MIDI settings (TE's): key 2 lets MIDI in, which the app's MIX, PLAY and PERFORM need
SCREEN+DIAL 1
turns the app's pages · not yet tried on a unit
PATTERN 1 → 2
chained odd then even: the playhead is white on A, purple on B v3

IF IT DOES NOT START

Switch off (or let the battery run out). Hold SCREEN, switch on. Install the version you had, or TE 1.2.45. Galax-z never writes the part that runs update mode.
